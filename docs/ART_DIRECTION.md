# ART DIRECTION

**Status:** DRAFT

## Initial Pillars

- corporate futurism
- monumental architecture
- clean and controlled environments
- minimal conventional cyberpunk neon
- restrained signage
- architectural scale over prop density
- atmospheric depth

## Direction

Primary forms should communicate scale before surface detail does. Lighting and materials should support architecture, depth, and controlled atmosphere rather than turning every space into a neon spectacle.
