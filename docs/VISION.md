# VISION

**Status:** DRAFT

## Project Fantasy

MONOLITH is a first-person exploration experience set inside a monumental corporate megastructure.

> The player is a human inside architecture built at a scale that feels almost inhuman.

## Player Experience

The player should feel physically small, curious, slightly intimidated by the architecture, and drawn toward distant spaces and vistas. Exploration, observation, atmosphere, architecture, sound, and spatial progression are the focus.

## Vertical Slice Philosophy

The project is intentionally narrow. A small number of spaces should be polished to a very high standard rather than building a large quantity of content. The world may imply a vast city and megastructure while only a limited number of areas are fully playable.

## Visual Goals

- monumental futuristic architecture
- towers approximately 800–1200 m tall
- strong contrast between human-scale objects and megastructure-scale forms
- high graphical fidelity
- atmospheric depth
- controlled corporate environments
- memorable vistas
- restrained visual language

## Non-Goals

The current vertical slice does not aim to include combat, weapons, multiplayer, vehicles, crafting, skill trees, procedural city generation, complex NPC AI, dialogue trees, large inventories, or open-world systems.
